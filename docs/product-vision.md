# LearnTogether

> Helping every child learn at their own pace.

---

# Vision

LearnTogether is an offline-first educational platform designed to help children develop literacy, numeracy, communication, and everyday life skills through engaging, accessible, and distraction-free learning experiences.

The project began with one learner in mind—my younger brother. Like many children with cerebral palsy and speech impairments, he learns differently. Existing learning applications were either filled with advertisements, locked behind subscriptions, or not designed around his learning style.

LearnTogether exists to change that.

Our goal is to create an educational experience that is simple, enjoyable, accessible, and available to every child regardless of internet access.

---

# Mission

To empower children with diverse learning needs by providing an educational platform that adapts to their pace, celebrates every achievement, and removes unnecessary barriers to learning.

---

# Problem Statement

Many educational apps:

- Require internet access
- Display distracting advertisements
- Hide content behind subscriptions
- Assume all children learn at the same pace
- Lack accessibility features
- Are not designed for children with motor or speech impairments

Parents and caregivers often struggle to find affordable educational tools that can be used consistently at home.

---

# Solution

LearnTogether provides:

- Offline learning
- Large accessible interfaces
- Audio-assisted lessons
- Visual learning experiences
- Positive reinforcement
- Progress tracking
- Caregiver tools
- Expandable learning modules

---

# Core Principles

## Accessibility First

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn regardless of ability.

---

## Offline First

Learning should not depend on internet connectivity.

---

## Simplicity

Every screen should have one clear objective.

---

## Positive Reinforcement

Children should be encouraged instead of punished.

---

## Consistency

Predictable layouts help children build confidence.

---

## Privacy

Children's data belongs to their families.

No advertising.

No unnecessary tracking.
